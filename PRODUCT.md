# Kreatix UI community

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Motion, Hono, and SQLite WAL on Node 24. Fixed-format HTML and React component previews. Fancy Components' MIT-licensed interactions are used with attribution.

## Users

Developers looking for good free UI libraries and components, and small independent creators who struggle to get their own UI work discovered.

## Product Purpose

A community for sharing and discovering UI libraries and individual components, not a single component library maintained by Kreatix.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The platform and listed work must be fully open-source. Nothing may be paid, ever. No paid components, paid listings, premium platform features, or paid visibility. The selected address is ui.kreatix.dev. Existing root domains contain important services and must remain untouched. Implementation and the first deployment were explicitly requested on 2026-10-12. The first version is deployed on the existing VPS without DNS changes.

## Interface commitments

Lead with the gallery, not a marketing headline or filler. Morphs must explain related states and hierarchy, never blanket blur or animate the entire page. Preserve reduced-motion preferences. Use at least 16px text in form controls to prevent iOS focus zoom, disable accidental selection and double-tap zoom on chrome, and preserve text/code selection and accessible pinch zoom.

## Brand Commitments

Uses the user's existing Kreatix domain. Final community display name remains open.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's conversation establishes the audience, community purpose, free-only rule, and selected subdomain. No actual submissions, creator statistics, testimonials, or new community assets have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Free and open-source permanently, not a launch promotion.
- Help small creators get discovered without buying exposure.
- Treat components and whole libraries as first-class contributions.
- Do not confuse community discovery with a proprietary library or a paid marketplace.
